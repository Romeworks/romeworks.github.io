Tindero

Your sari-sari store's record book, with one camera.

Coming soon to Google Play

What it does

  • Sell with one camera. Hold an item in the box: barcodes ring up at once, and items without a barcode (eggs, ice, candies, per-stick cigarettes) are recognized from your own product photos.
  • Add products fast. The camera reads the barcode and the name on the label for you.
  • Utang per customer, with payments and history.
  • Today's report: sales, profit, best sellers and what is running low.
  • Built for one hand, works offline, in English or Filipino, and in your own currency.
