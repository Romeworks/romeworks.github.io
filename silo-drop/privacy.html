Silo Drop Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026

Silo Drop is a single-player arcade game by Romeworks. This policy explains what the app does with information, which is very little.

What the app collects

Nothing. Silo Drop does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. It has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no network access of any kind. The app does not request the internet permission.

What the app stores on your device

Your deepest ledge, your salvage, which moves you have learned, and whether sound is on are saved in the app's private storage on your device, so the game can pick up where you left off. None of it leaves the device, and it is removed when the app is uninstalled.

Permissions

The app asks only to use the phone's vibration, for a short buzz when you are hit or set a new record. It uses no other permission.

Third parties

The app includes no third-party software development kits and shares no information with anyone. Google Play may collect information about your download and use of the app under Google's own privacy policy; that is separate from this app and outside its control.

Children

The app is suitable for a general audience. Because it collects no information, it collects no information from children.

Changes

If the app ever begins to collect information, for example by showing ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: romeworks.dev@gmail.com
