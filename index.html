Endless run / One thumb

Core Courier

A one-thumb endless arcade game you can play offline. The courier never stops running.

  • Drag anywhere to steer. Tap to dash, or flick to dash that way.
  • A dash cuts straight through a drone. Anything else that touches you ends the run.
  • A fresh arena every run, five sectors, instant restarts. No accounts, no sign-in.

Store app / Sari-sari stores

Tindero

Your sari-sari store's record book, with one camera.

  • Sell with one camera. Barcodes ring up at once, and items without one are recognized from your own product photos.
  • Utang per customer, with payments and history.

03 Studio

Small, fast things for the phone in your hand.

Romeworks is an independent studio making games and apps for Android. Everything is designed around one hand and a few spare seconds, and each one gets a look of its own.

  1. 01

    One thumb

    Every control sits under one thumb, so it works standing in line or with a bag in the other hand.

  2. 02

    Plays offline

    No signal, no problem. The games run without a connection and Tindero keeps its records on the phone.

  3. 03

    No accounts

    Nothing to sign up for. Open it and go.

  4. 04

    Its own look

    Each game and app gets its own colors and type, so none of them feels like a reskin of the last.
